--- B. NON-WEATHER LOSSES (inverter failure, soiling, shading, grid outage) ---
Assumptions: 5% of customers per year hit; each loses 10-40% of annual generation.
  Customers hit per year: 493 of 10000 | average loss per hit customer: Rs 6305
  Total non-weather loss per year: Rs 0.31 crore = 0.3 x the whole premium pool (Rs 1.07 crore)
  Non-weather events cause 52% of all customer savings shortfall; none of it is paid by this product.
  Customer-years with a material loss and no payout rise from 7.2% (weather only) to 44.8% (with non-weather losses).
